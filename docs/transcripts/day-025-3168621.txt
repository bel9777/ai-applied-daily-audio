Alex: Where do people get burned here? What are the failure modes?
Jordan: The biggest trap is failing to specify an AND condition. Copilot will default to an OR test and swamp your review with tiny lines. The second risk is ungrounded commentary. You control this by recalculating the math yourself and verifying every driver against the source.
